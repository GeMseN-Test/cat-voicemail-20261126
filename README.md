## The line

> You've reached Beans. Your mug's on the floor — 4 a.m. felt right. Leave a message. I'll sit on it.

99 characters. One voicemail greeting, recorded by a cat called Beans.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. That is the whole program.

## Why this line

- **Voicemail first:** opens with "You've reached Beans" and closes with "Leave a message" — it is a greeting, not a caption about a cat.
- **One concrete cat crime:** the mug on the floor at 4 a.m. — knocking things off shelves at 4 a.m. is the most cat-specific joke there is.
- **A character, not a narrator:** Beans speaks, admits it ("4 a.m. felt right"), and judges the caller at the end ("I'll sit on it") — the message itself gets sat on.
- **Works spoken aloud:** short sentences, punch at the very end, no wordplay that needs reading twice.
- **Fresh, not a tired pun:** no "leave a message after the meow", no "paws the phone" — the joke is a behaviour (knocking, sitting), not a cat pun.
